## In brief

- We do **not use tracking cookies** or analytics tools by default.
- Fonts and images are hosted on our own server. Visiting the website does not send data to Google or other third parties.
- External content such as maps or videos only loads with your consent.
- The members area is only used by invited members.

## Local storage and consent

We store your choice in the consent dialog in your browser (localStorage) so that we do not have to ask again on every visit. This is technically necessary (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer.

We only use all other services with your consent (§ 25(1) TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

## External content

If you consent or load an item individually, a connection to the respective provider is established. The provider receives at least your IP address.

- **OpenStreetMap** (map on the contact page): OpenStreetMap Foundation, St John’s Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom. [Privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy). An EU adequacy decision exists for the United Kingdom.
- **YouTube** (if videos are embedded), in privacy-enhanced mode via youtube-nocookie.com: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. [Privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Links to Instagram and Telegram are ordinary links. Data is only sent to these services when you click the link.

## Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your details to answer your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete messages once they have been dealt with and no retention obligations apply.

Our email addresses on the website are protected against automated harvesting. Decryption takes place entirely in your browser; no data is transmitted.

## Newsletter

For the newsletter we need your email address and your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We use the double opt-in procedure: you receive an email with a confirmation link and are only added after confirming. We log the time and IP address of sign-up and confirmation to be able to prove consent.

Mailing service: [PLEASE ADD: provider, registered office, link to privacy policy]. A data processing agreement is in place with the provider.

You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every issue or by email.

## Members area

For the members area we use **Supabase** (Supabase Inc., 970 Toa Payoh North #07-04, Singapore 318992). Data is stored in a data centre in the EU (Frankfurt am Main). A data processing agreement including EU standard contractual clauses is in place with Supabase.

We process: email address, password (only as an encrypted hash), profile details you enter yourself and login times. The legal basis is your membership of the association (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). After logging in, your browser stores a session token (localStorage). This is technically necessary to keep you logged in and is deleted when you log out.

## Public profiles in the network

On the profile pages under “Network” we show the name, profile text, focus areas, links and, where applicable, a portrait of members. This is based on the person’s consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): profiles from the members area only appear if the person chooses “show publicly” and the board has approved the profile. Consent can be withdrawn at any time in the members area or by email; the profile is then removed with the next website update (within 24 hours at the latest).

Portraits from the members area are stored with Supabase (Frankfurt am Main) and copied to our server when the website is built. Visiting the website does not send any data to Supabase.

## Audience measurement

Whether we count visits is up to you: statistics only run if they are set up and you consent to the “Statistics” category (§ 25(1) TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We use [PLEASE ADD if activated: cookie-free Matomo on our own server / Plausible Analytics, EU]. The IP address is truncated and no profiles are created across websites. We need the figures for reports to our funders.

## Donations

If you donate by bank transfer, our bank sends us your name, IBAN, the amount and the reference. We process this data to record the donation and, on request, to issue a donation receipt (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR). We keep it for as long as required by tax retention periods (usually ten years).

The EPC QR code on the donation page is generated on our server. Scanning it does not send any data to us. If you donate online via a donation service provider, their privacy policy also applies.

## Membership administration

We process members’ data (name, address, contact details, fee data) to administer the membership (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and keep it after leaving for as long as required by tax and association law.

## Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is: Die Sächsische Datenschutz- und Transparenzbeauftragte, Devrientstraße 5, 01067 Dresden, [www.datenschutz.sachsen.de](https://www.datenschutz.sachsen.de).
