## Our aim

Dance is for everyone – and so is our website. We follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. Every change to the website is checked for barriers automatically.

## What we do

- The whole website can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
- Text has sufficient contrast. You can enlarge the text without losing content.
- Images have text descriptions. Headings and regions are marked up for screen readers.
- If you have enabled “reduce motion” in your operating system, animations are switched off.
- Email addresses are protected against spam and can be revealed with a keyboard or screen reader.
- There is a page in Leichte Sprache (easy-to-read German).

## Known limitations

- The network animation on the homepage is decorative. All content is also available as text.
- External content (e.g. the OpenStreetMap map) is beyond our control.
- PDF documents such as the membership form and statutes are not yet fully accessible. [PLEASE CHECK]
- We do not yet have videos in sign language.

## About this statement

This statement is based on a self-assessment and automated tests (axe-core).
