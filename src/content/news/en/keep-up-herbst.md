KEEP UP is TanzNetzDresden's professional training. Since 2022 we have been training several times a week at TENZA, with teachers from Dresden and guests from elsewhere.

In autumn the classes change every week. The Open Studio remains open for free training.

## Workshop in November

On 14 November there is a workshop from 11 am to 3 pm. Registration is via the Telegram group.
