Studio Round is our format for work in progress. Three artists each show 15 minutes from a piece that does not have to be finished. Then we open the room for a moderated conversation with the audience.

Since 2024 the series has been running across regions. Together with Tanznetz Freiburg and ID Frankfurt we invite each other, funded by the Impulsförderung of the Nationales Performance Netz.

> We want to see what others are working on right now, not just what they have finished.
>
> — Alina Lucifero · Project lead Studio Round

For the ninth edition, guests travel from Freiburg and Frankfurt. From Dresden, an artist from the network shows first scenes of her new work.

Admission is free. Donations go directly towards the guests' travel costs.
