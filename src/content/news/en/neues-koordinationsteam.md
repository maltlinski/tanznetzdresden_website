At the general meeting we elected a new coordination team. Four dance artists from the network share the work of the board.

- **Alina Lucifero** Regional partnerships, public relations, project lead Studio Round
- **Rika Yotsumoto** Finances, working group coordination, project lead KEEP UP
- **Justine Rouquart** Membership administration, project lead POP UP and Bodies & Textiles
- **Malte Leonard Herz** National partnerships, project lead Dresdance Gala and vision:danceable

The working groups remain the heart of the association. If you want to get involved, the working groups are the most direct way into the network.
