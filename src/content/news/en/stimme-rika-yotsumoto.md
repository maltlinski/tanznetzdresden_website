## How did KEEP UP start?

In 2022 there was no regular professional training for the independent scene in Dresden. We started with two sessions a week; today there are three to five.

## What has changed since then?

More guests come from elsewhere, and many people who are new in town find their way into the network through training.

> Training is where the scene meets every week. That is where the network begins.
>
> — Rika Yotsumoto

## What do you hope for in the coming years?

More reliable planning for the teachers, and a space that is permanently ours.
