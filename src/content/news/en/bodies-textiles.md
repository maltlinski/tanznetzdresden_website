How does a body move in fabric, and what does clothing tell us about movement? With Bodies & Textiles we are launching a format in 2027 that brings dance and sustainable fashion together.

Our partners are Wir Gestalten Dresden and the Slow Fashion Festival. We are planning workshops, an open lab and a presentation as part of the festival.

If you would like to join the working group, get in touch via our [contact page](/en/contact/).
