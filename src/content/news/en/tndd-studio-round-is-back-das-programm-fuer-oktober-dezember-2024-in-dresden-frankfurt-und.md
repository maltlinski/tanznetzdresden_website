*Our calls for entries for the next three editions of TNDD studio round were successful and that's why we can reveal to you today who will be taking part in the three editions of TNDD studio round in Dresden, Frankfurt am Main and Freiburg in October and December. All the others in the following blog post.*

We are pleased to announce that the exchange and presentation format TNDD studio round of TanzNetzDresden in cooperation with [ID\_Tanzhaus Frankfurt Rhein-Main](https://tanzhausfrm.de/) and [Tanznetz Freiburg](https://tanznetz-freiburg.de/) will go on tour through three German cities in the fourth quarter of 2024.

Specifically, the following three dates will present artists at three locations who have already been selected in a completed application phase:

- **October 25, 2024, 8 p.m.**: **Eva Krause** (Freiburg), **Felix Chang** (Frankfurt am Main) & **Romy Schwarzer** (Dresden) [TENZA](http://www.tenza.de/), Dresden (mit anschließendem Besuch von POP UP exchange am 26.10.2024 in Dresden)
- **December 06, 2024:  Marion Plantey** (Frankfurt am Main)**, Sophia Barr** (Freiburg) & **Joao Pedro de Paula** (Dresden), [Südufer](https://suedufer-freiburg.de/), Freiburg
- **December 13, 2024: Cindy Hammer** (Dresden)**, Karolin Stächele** (Freiburg) & **Marie Kobzeva** (Frankfurt am Main), [Z-Zentrum für Proben und Forschung](https://kultur-frankfurt.de/portal/de/Buehne/Proberaeume/1920/2456/0/0/4.aspx), Frankfurt am Main

The TNDD studio round will once again function as a presentation and exchange format that will bring local artists into contact with artists from other cities.

We would like to explicitly point out that only one day after the edition in Dresden, namely on 26.10.2024, the POP UP exchange event, which is also open to the public, will take place at TENZA. This will also take place this year in cooperation with [ID\_Tanzhaus Frankfurt Rhein-Main](https://tanzhausfrm.de/), [Tanznetz Freiburg](https://tanznetz-freiburg.de/) and [tanz\_netzwerk\_bremen](https://lafdk-bremen.de/).

Further information on this date is available at:  
[https://tanznetzdresden.de/pop-up-exchange](/#formate)
