For more than 15 years, the independent dance scene in Dresden has organised itself in TanzNetzDresden. To keep it that way, we have once again applied for institutional funding for 2027 from the Office for Culture and Monument Protection.

## What it contains

The application describes our ongoing formats: KEEP UP training, Studio Round, POP UP, vision:danceable and International Dance Day. Bodies & Textiles joins them in 2027.

A large share of the funds goes to fees for dance artists, to studio rent and to the public relations work that makes the network visible.

> Funding for the network is funding for more than 70 dance artists.
>
> — Coordination team TanzNetzDresden

We will share the decision here and in our newsletter.
