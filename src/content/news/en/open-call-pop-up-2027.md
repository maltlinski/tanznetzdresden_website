Since 2019, POP UP has been our low-threshold performance format. Dance artists from the network show new work, first drafts and pieces that need a place to be tried out.

## Who we are looking for

We welcome applications from dancers, choreographers and collectives from Dresden and the region. The piece may be up to 20 minutes long and does not have to be finished.

## What we offer

Stage, technical support and a rehearsal on site. The POP UP working group accompanies the preparation and is there for all your questions.

## How to apply

Send us a short description of your project, a link to a rehearsal video and your contact details by 15 November. You will find the email address on our [contact page](/en/contact/).
