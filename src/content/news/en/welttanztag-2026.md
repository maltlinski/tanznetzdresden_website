Every year on International Dance Day we leave the studios. On 29 April, dance artists from the network danced in several places around the city, without a stage and without tickets.

Many passers-by stopped, some joined in. That is what it is about for us: showing dance where it does not usually appear in everyday life.

Thank you to everyone involved and to the International Dance Day working group, who organised the day.
