## +++ Extended calls for POP UP exchange & TNDD studio round 2024 open calls until September 10, 2024 (en) +++

*The two calls for entries for the upcoming editions of POP UP exchange and TNDD studio round have been extended and will now end on September 10. So all of you who want to take advantage of this special opportunity to be part of one of the participating cities should take your chance now. (scroll up for german version)*

All information on the POP UP exchange open call can be found under this link:  
[https://tanznetzdresden.de/pop-up-exchange-ausschreibung-gestartet-open-call-started-date-26-10-2024-de-eng](/news/pop-up-exchange-ausschreibung-gestartet-open-call-started-date-26-10-2024-de-eng/)

All information on the TNDD studio round open call can be found under this link:  
[https://tanznetzdresden.de/ausschreibungen-gestartet-open-calls-started-tndd-studio-round-de-eng](/news/ausschreibungen-gestartet-open-calls-started-tndd-studio-round-de-eng/)

More information is available under:

[**Here**](/#formate) you find more about TNDD studio round.

TNDD studio round is a project by TanzNetzDresden. Funded by the Office for Culture and Monument Protection and the Cultural Foundation of the Free State of Saxony. The measure is co-financed by tax funds on the basis of the budget passed by the Saxon State Parliament.

Supported by the NATIONAL PERFORMANCE NETZ Impulsförderung Tanz & Theater, funded by the Federal Government Commissioner for Culture and the Media and the Ministries of Culture and the Arts of the federal states.
